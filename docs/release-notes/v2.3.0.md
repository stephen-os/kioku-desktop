# Kioku v2.3.0

**Release Date:** October 12, 2026

This release rebuilds Courses around course-owned content and re-architects
text-to-speech for instant, offline playback, alongside a long list of
reliability and security fixes.

---

## Highlights

### Courses, rebuilt
- **Course-owned content.** A course now holds its own copies of its decks,
  quizzes, and notes, independent of your library.
- **Study in context.** Study decks, take quizzes, and read notes from inside a
  course; completion and best scores are tracked per lesson item.
- **Lesson gating.** Later lessons stay locked until earlier ones are complete,
  with an optional "open navigation" mode that disables gating.
- **Adopt.** Save a personal, editable copy of any course deck, quiz, or note to
  your library with one click.
- **In-place authoring.** Edit course content directly, author blank decks /
  quizzes / notes, or copy existing library items into a course.
- **On your library pages.** Unlocked course items surface read-only under "From
  your courses," with a Due filter and a grid/list view toggle.
- **Import & export.** JSON import/export for whole courses (course-owned), with
  an Export button on the course page. The import schema is documented in Help.

### Text-to-speech, re-architected
- **Install-time, opt-in voices.** Voice models download once at install, by
  language (English recommended) — no fetching while you study.
- **Pre-generated, bounded memory.** Audio is baked in the background and cached
  to disk; studying just plays the cached files, so no model stays resident.
- **Per-side language.** Choose the spoken language for the front and back of a
  card independently.
- **Fallbacks & control.** Falls back to your system voice until audio is baked;
  clear cached audio anytime from Settings.
- First use of read-aloud downloads the voice engine and your selected languages
  (one-time); until then Kioku uses your system voice.

### Notes
- `[[wiki links]]` render as clickable links between pages.
- Code blocks inside notes are syntax-highlighted.

---

## Fixes & hardening
- Per-user ownership is enforced in the backend; profiles whose passwords predate
  the Argon2 migration can sign in again (verified once and upgraded in place).
- The voice engine download is verified against a pinned SHA-256 over HTTPS, and
  archive extraction is size/entry-capped.
- Course content writes and the first-run demo seed are now atomic; the voice
  engine can no longer hang the app on a stalled request.
- Image imports are path-confined and extension-allowlisted; page search escapes
  `LIKE` wildcards.
- Removed the unused file-based image-attachment subsystem (notes keep inline
  images).
